How it works

From a bare SKU to an approved record in five moves

Every step is visible and every output is reviewable. The same pipeline runs when someone clicks a button and when the nightly job runs on its own.

  1. 1

    Collect

    Loads the product, its category, brand, barcodes and any datasheet links already on file.

  2. 2

    Research

    Searches the web, looks up barcodes, and reads PDF and Word datasheets with an AI model.

  3. 3

    Write

    Generates the name, bullets, long description, specs, tags and SEO text using the prompt rules of that category.

  4. 4

    Check

    Keeps the brand only if the original named it, normalizes units and grammar, and removes unsafe markup.

  5. 5

    Approve

    Lands in a queue where an approver edits, accepts or rejects, with the audit trail kept.

Capabilities

What a catalog team writes by hand, delivered as a reviewed first draft

Product copy

Name, short and long description, specs and tags, written for the category and in Spanish-first style, with rules you can change.

Datasheet reading

Pulls specifications out of PDF and DOCX files, with limits on size, pages and cost per product.

Image discovery

Finds candidate photos, scores relevance and transparency, labels quality, then prepares and stores the chosen ones.

Taxonomy-aware prompts

A general prompt plus overrides by department, category or subcategory: tone, format and key attributes to always mention.

Warranty text

Standardizes free-form warranty descriptions into one consistent, customer-friendly format.

Nightly enrichment

Pick the taxonomies to cover; each night new matching products are enriched and queued for review.

Approval queue

Pending, approved and rejected items with editing, one pending item per product, and who decided what.

Notifications

In-app alerts and optional email to the people who approve, with a deep link to the queue.

Cache & dedupe

Results are cached for an hour and identical simultaneous requests share one run instead of paying twice.

        Connect

        One engine, any CRM or storefront, through external modules

        The engine exposes a small REST API. A connector module is a thin piece of code you own that asks for enrichment, waits for approval and copies the result into your system.

        Pull

        Your system asks for a product by id and receives the proposal immediately.

        Poll & sync

        A scheduled module reads approved items and writes them into the CRM or store.

        Batch

        Loop a CSV of SKUs through the same endpoint and review the results in one queue.

        Safe by default

        Built to sit in front of real catalog data

        • API key on every route

          In production the service refuses to start without keys, unless you explicitly choose to run open.

        • Outbound requests are fenced

          Fetching images, PDFs and pages checks the real destination address on every connection and redirect, so it cannot be pointed at your internal network.

        • Generated HTML is cleaned

          Model output and reviewer edits keep only simple formatting tags; scripts, handlers and links are removed.

        • Roles decide, not request bodies

          Who approved something comes from a trusted header and is checked against the database, never from the payload.

        • Limits everywhere

          Size caps on downloads and prompts, an image pixel limit, and an explicit CORS allow-list.
